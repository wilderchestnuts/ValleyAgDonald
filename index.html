How Degree Days Work

Filbertworm development only occurs above 50°F. Each day contributes:
DD = (Tmax + Tmin) / 2 − 50 (floored at 0)

Accumulated from April 1, degree days predict when moths fly, eggs hatch, and larvae enter nuts — enabling precision spray timing.

Trap Placement Guidelines

  • Delta trap baited with C. latiferreana pheromone lure
  • Hang in upper ⅓ of canopy — pheromone vapor is heavier than air
  • 4 traps / first 10 ac; +1 trap per 4 ac beyond 10 ac
  • Replace lure every 30 days
  • Check weekly; record total moths per trap
  • Set traps by mid-to-late May to catch first flights

Spray Timing

  • Target: eggs and wandering larvae before nut entry
  • Spray ~7–10 days after first large flight catch
  • Or at 1,023 DD from April 1 biofix (peak egg hatch)
  • Mating disruption: "trap shutdown" = dispensers working
  • Consult your certified crop adviser for product selection
